FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Nightline?

Nightline is a web app that turns every night trip into a plan with a safety net. You tell it where you are going and when you will arrive, and your trusted adult is told only if you miss your check-in.

How does a check-in work?

You open the app, take a quick selfie and confirm. In this prototype the selfie is not compared with your face yet. Real face matching is planned for the next phase.

What happens if I miss a check-in?

You get a first alert, then a second alert five minutes later. If there is still no action, your trip details go to your trusted adult. If the family does not act, an emergency message goes to the police or a helpline.

How do I stop the alerts?

Type your 4-digit PIN on the “I’m okay” screen. One entry ends the whole alert chain.

Who can see my location?

Only the people you choose, and only after check-ins are missed. This prototype does not read your device location. It uses the places you type in.

Are the alerts real?

No. In this prototype alerts, SMS and emergency messages are simulated on screen and nothing is sent. Real delivery is part of the roadmap.

Where is my data stored?

In this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. You can delete everything from Settings at any time.
